Insurance Claim Assistance in New York State

Documentation, scope-of-loss reporting and direct adjuster coordination for a faster claim.

The restoration work and the insurance claim move on parallel tracks, and the biggest delays we see happen when those two tracks aren't communicating. Missing documentation, an incomplete scope of loss, or a homeowner unsure what their policy actually covers can stall a claim for weeks.

Empire State Restoration documents every loss with dated photos, moisture readings and a detailed scope of work from the first inspection, then communicates directly with your insurance adjuster so the claim and the physical restoration stay in sync. For a general understanding of your rights during a claim dispute, NY Department of Financial Services publishes consumer guidance for New York policyholders.

Common Causes of Insurance Claim Assistance

  • Incomplete or missing loss documentation
  • Confusion over what a policy actually covers
  • Delayed or unresponsive adjuster communication
  • Disputes over scope of repair versus replacement
  • Uncertainty about deductibles and out-of-pocket costs

Signs You Need Insurance Claim Assistance Now

  • An adjuster requesting documentation you don't have
  • Uncertainty whether flood, sewer backup or mold is covered
  • A claim that has stalled for more than two weeks
  • A settlement offer that seems to underscope the damage

Our Insurance Claim Assistance Process

Every job starts with a documented inspection, moves through extraction or removal, then structural drying or repair, and closes with a final walkthrough and insurance paperwork if applicable — see our full restoration process for the step-by-step breakdown we follow on every call.

Serving All of New York State

New York homeowners insurance policies vary widely in how they treat sewer backup, mold and flood exclusions. We recommend reviewing your declarations page annually and consulting NY Department of Financial Services if you believe a claim has been unfairly delayed or denied — we're glad to provide our own documentation to support that process. Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge extra for insurance coordination?

Insurance documentation and adjuster coordination is included as part of our standard restoration process, not billed as a separate service.

What if my claim is denied?

We provide detailed documentation you can use to appeal or dispute a denial, and can point you to resources like NY Department of Financial Services for formal complaint processes if needed.
