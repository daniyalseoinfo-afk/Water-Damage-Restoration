Water Damage Restoration in New York State

Emergency extraction and structural drying for burst pipes, appliance failures and roof leaks.

A water loss rarely announces itself politely. A supply line lets go behind a washing machine, a radiator fitting corrodes through, or an ice dam backs water under a roof deck, and within minutes it is moving through subfloor, drywall and insulation faster than most homeowners realize. The first 24 to 48 hours determine whether a loss stays a drying job or turns into a mold and structural-repair job.

Empire State Restoration treats water damage restoration as a race against the clock. Dispatch teams are staged across New York so a technician can begin extraction and moisture mapping quickly, then hold the structure at target drying benchmarks with commercial dehumidification until every reading confirms dry — not just visually dry.

Common Causes of Water Damage Restoration

  • Burst or frozen supply pipes
  • Washing machine and dishwasher hose failures
  • Water heater and boiler leaks
  • Roof and flashing intrusion
  • HVAC condensate line backups
  • Ice dam melt under roofing

Signs You Need Water Damage Restoration Now

  • Discoloration or staining on ceilings and walls
  • A persistent musty or damp odor
  • Warped, cupped or soft flooring
  • Peeling paint or bubbling drywall
  • Higher-than-normal humidity in one area of the home

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon should water extraction start after a pipe bursts?

Extraction should begin as soon as it is safe to enter the property, ideally within a few hours. Waiting longer than 24-48 hours significantly increases the risk of mold growth and permanent material damage.

Can you dry a structure without tearing out all the drywall?

Often, yes. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to target only the materials that are actually saturated, which keeps demolition and rebuild costs down when the structure allows it.
